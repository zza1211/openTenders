1. BACKGROUND 
 
The National Lotteries Commission (NLC) is a public entity established by Lotteries Act No. 57 of 
1997, as amended to regulate the South African lotteries industry. The functions of the Commission 
can be divided into two categories, namely “regulation of National Lottery and other Lotteries” and 
“administration of the National Lottery Distribution Trust Fund (NLDTF)”. 
 
The NLC has a dual mandate, namely “regulation of National Lottery and other Lotteries” and 
“administration of the NLDTF”. The Distributing Agencies (DAs), appointed by the Minister of Trade 
& Industry are responsible for the adjudication of the funding applications as per the Lotteries Act and 
applicable Regulations. 
 
The NLC operates in nine provinces with the head office located at Hatfield in the Gauteng province. 
A private Multi-Protocol Label Switching (MPLS) WAN network interconnects each of the NLC 
provincial offices to the Head Office in Hatfield. A central server room is situated at the Hatfield office 
and houses all the core ICT network infrastructure. The central server room also houses all other on-
premise ICT infrastructure required for NLC business operations. This MPLS network caters for both 
Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) telephony services as well as data network services. 
 
The NLC seeks to upgrade its local area network (LAN) infrastructure based on modern high 
availability and secure network switch technology. The envisaged LAN solution seeks to achieve a 
combination of the following features and functionality: 
i. 
Network port performance: 
a. 1 Gb/s to end device 
b. 10 Gb/s connectivity of the LAN backbone at Hatfield. 
c. 40 Gb/s connectivity from core switches to the aggregation switch 
ii. 
Network switch configuration capabilities: 
a. VLAN segmentation of LAN traffic. 
b. 1 Gb/s ethernet ports with PoE. 
c. 40Gb/s uplink ports 
d. Virtual stacking. 
e. QoS (quality of service): 
i. Email traffic. 
ii. Http/https traffic. 
iii. VoIP traffic. 
iv. CCTV traffic. 
v. Other traffic.

[Page 4]
iii. 
Wifi access connectivity 
a. Wifi 6 connectivity. 
b. Corporate access profile. 
c. Guest access profile. 
d. BYOD access profile 
iv. 
Cybersecurity: 
a. Unified Threat Management (UTM) 
b. Intrusion prevention 
c. Radius authentication for network devices 
v. 
Firewall and DMZ 
vi. 
Dashboard and Reporting: 
a. Analytics 
 
In line with the requirements of the Public Finance Management Act (PFMA) No. 1 of 1999, as 
amended and Treasury Regulations, the NLC invites suitably qualified and experienced service 
providers to supply, install, configure and integrate a LAN network for the NLC.

3. PURPOSE OF THE TERMS OF REFERENCE 
 
The purpose of the terms of reference (TOR) is to appoint a qualified and experienced 
service provider to supply, install, configure and integrate a LAN network for the NLC for a 
period of five (5) years. 
 
4. OBJECTIVES 
Deliver, install and configure a LAN solution, the associated services and requisite 
infrastructure at each of the nine (9) provincial offices. 
 
5. SCOPE OF WORK 
 
The solution entails the provision of the following services or capabilities:  
1. Supply and install new cabling infrastructure at the Hatfield premises: 
1.1. Optic fibre cables between server room and patch rooms at Head Office 
2. Supply, install, configure, and integrate the components of the proposed LAN solution: 
2.1. Network switches at all NLC offices

[Page 6]
2.2. Wi-Fi access points at all NLC offices 
2.3. Integrate proposed LAN infrastructure to the WAN network 
2.4. License all the equipment comprising the proposed LAN solution 
3. Implement a LAN dashboard platform for ICT personnel to monitor each of the core 
LAN components i.e.: 
3.1. LAN uptime. 
3.2. LAN performance metrics 
3.3. LAN Cybersecurity threat metrics 
3.4. Reporting capabilities 
4. Render support to and maintenance of the LAN infrastructure 
4.1. Technical support and troubleshooting 
4.2. Maintain and update all requisite hardware and firmware licences 
4.3. Firmware updates 
4.4. Replacement of faulty equipment
