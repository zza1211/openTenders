SECTION I
 INVITATION FOR BIDS (IFB)
 1.
DPA-II, Ministry of External Affairs, New Delhi invites sealed bids in two bid
system in two parts from eligible bidders for setting up a Centre of Excellence in
Information Technology(CEIT)  on CIF basis at San Jose, Costa Rica and Roseau,
Commonwealth of Dominica(CD) each, and upgradation of existing CEIT in Panama as
per Schedule of Requirements (Section IV).
 1.1.
The tender is in two parts. Eligible bidders can participate in one or both of the
parts  independently,  subject  to  the  fulfilment  of  the  specified  eligibility  criteria.
Evaluation of proposals shall be done separately and independently for both parts.
 1.2.
Part-A of tender pertains  to Training  which includes  deployment  of trainers,
designing and supply of course content and overall management of IT centres at San
Jose, Costa Rica; Roseau, Commonwealth of Dominica (CD) and Panama City, Panama,
as per Schedule of Requirements (Section IV).
 1.3.
Part-B of tender pertains to IT infrastructure and requires supply, installation,
testing, commissioning and maintenance of Hardware and Software of IT centres at San
Jose, Costa Rica; Roseau, Commonwealth of Dominica (CD) and Panama City, Panama,
as per Schedule of Requirements (Section IV).

4. Eligiblity Criteria
4.1 For Part-A ( Training)
4.1.1.
Bidder must be a company/firm incorporated in India. 
4.1.2.
The bidder shall be into the field of IT training and shall have minimum
average annual turnover of Rs 2.5 crore during the last three financial years. 
4.1.3.
The bidder must have imparted training for minimum Rs 2 crore in any one
year OR minimum Rs 1.4 crore per annum in any two years OR minimum Rs 1
crore per annum in any three years during last seven years period. To ensure
the verifiability of the above, revenue earned by training imparted to the Govt
of  India/State  Govt/PSUs   only,  shall  be  considered  for  determining  the
eligibility.
OR
The bidder shall have experience of setting of at least one IT Centre
involving training also, of value not less than Rs. 8 crores or two IT Centres
involving training also, of value not less than Rs.5 crores each or  three IT
Centres involving training also, of value not less than Rs.4 crores each. The
bidder must have executed at least one such project overseas. 
4.1.4. Relevant ISO certificate in IT training field
4.1.5. The  trainers  shall  have  good  academic  background  with  B.E/
B.Tech/MCA/M.Sc in Computers/IT/Electronics with atleast 60% marks, and
having  atleast  5  years  IT/Training  experience.  The  trainers  shall  be  on
company rolls for at least last 1 year and shall be certified on at least one
relevant technology platforms viz. Microsoft, Cisco, Oracle and Linux etc. 
4.1.6. The  proposed  centre-head  shall  possess  BE/B.Tech/MCA/  MSc  in
Computer/  IT/Electronics  with  at  least  60%  marks,  alongwith
MTech/ME/MBA with at least 60% marks. The Center head sould have atleast
7 years IT/Training experience an shall be certified on at least one relevant
technology platform.viz. Microsoft, Cisco, Oracle and Linux etc. For Costa
Rica IT centre, Centre head must have fluency in Spanish and English.
4.1.7. The  bidder  shall  be  able  to  award  a certificate  of  proficiency  to  the
participants who complete the courses.
4.2 For Part B ( Physical IT Infrastructure )
10

[Page 11]
4.2.1. Bidder must be a company/firm incorporated in India. 
4.2.2. The bidders shall be in the business of IT i.e. IT Hardware, Software and
Networking, etc. and shall have minimum average annual turnover of Rs 10
crore during the last 3 financial years.
4.2.3. The bidder shall have experience of supply, installation, commissioning,
networking and maintenance of similar IT infrastructure to Govt of India/State
Govt/PSUs . The bidder must have successfully executed one such project of
value not less than Rs. 7 crore or two such projects of value not less than Rs. 4
crore each or 3 such projects of not less than Rs. 2.5 core each during last 7
years.
4.2.4. Relevant ISO certificate in IT infrastructure field.
4.2.5. Bidder shall be an authorised distributor/ resellers for major components
like PCs, Servers, Router, Switches, KVM Switches, UPS, Scanner, Printers.
4.2.6. The bidders shall have their own branch office/service centre in Costa
Rica ,Commonwealth of Dominica and Panama, separately or arrangement to
provide service through local dealer/service provider in these places on CIF
basis.
4.3 Bidders  shall  not  be  under  a  declaration  of  ineligibility  for  corrupt  and
fraudulent practices. 
4.4 Bidder  shall  be  registered  with  Sales  Tax/  Income  Tax  Department  of

 1  Scope of Work
 1.1  Government of India intends to set up an IT Centre for Training at San Jose, Costa
Rica and Roseau, Commonwealth of Dominica (CD); and up gradation of existing
CEIT at Panama City,Panama.
 1.1.1  The IT centre at San Jose, Costa Rica would have two class rooms for 25
students each, equipped with necessary IT infrastructure. This centre would have
provision of providing training to 600 students per year, for a period of two years.
 1.1.2  The IT centre at Roseau, Commonwealth of Dominica (CD)  would have
three class rooms for 25 students each, equipped with necessary IT infrastructure.
This centre would have provision of providing training to 500 students per year, for
a period of two years.
 1.1.3  The IT centre at Panama is already functioning with 4 Classrooms of 25
seats. Part of this infrastructure, as listed in SoR, needs to be upgraded .Training is
to be provided to local trainers for a period of three months. Training to other
students is not envisaged under the scope of this tender for this CEIT.
 1.1.4   It is expected that these CsEIT in time would run on self sustainable basis
thereafter. The courses to be covered are given in Schedule of requirement.
 1.2  The host government would be providing the following at their cost: 
 1.2.1  Requisite physical infrastructure including civil and electrical works, proper AC

 1.2.3  Necessary technical and administrative staff.
 1.2.4  Security of the premises.
 1.2.5  Exoneration from customs and other applicable taxes insofar import of equipment
from India or third country is concerned in connection with setting up IT Centre.
 1.2.6  Exemption from paying taxes etc. for Indian personnel deputed in connection with
setting/running up of the IT Centre.

 1.3.1.1  Designing and supply of content for course list in SoR.

International Standard Book Number (ISBN).

for a period of two years, the centre will be handed over to the host government.
