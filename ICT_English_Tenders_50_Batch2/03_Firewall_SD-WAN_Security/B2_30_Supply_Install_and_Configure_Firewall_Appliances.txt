SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION AND GENERAL PROPOSAL INFORMATION 
1. BACKGROUND OF COUNCIL FOR MEDICAL SCHEMES 
1.1. The Council for Medical Schemes is a statutory body established by the Medical Schemes Act (131 of 1998) to provide 
regulatory supervision of private health financing through medical schemes. Governance of the Council is vested in a board 
appointed by the Minister of Health, consisting of a non-executive Chairperson, Deputy Chairperson and 13 members. The 
executive head of the Council is the Registrar, also appointed by the Minister in terms of the Medical Schemes Act. The Council 
determines overall policy, but day to day decisions and management of staff are the responsibility of the Registrar and the 
Executive Managers. The Council supervises a massive and very important industry comprising more than 70 medical schemes 
registered in the country. 
 
2. VISION 
2.1. An efficient, effective, high-quality, agile and trusted South African regulator generating sustainable stakeholder value. 
 
3. MISSION  
 
3.1. Achieving our purpose of protecting the interests of members and beneficiaries by:  
• 
Controlling and co-ordinating the functioning and governance of all entities conducting the business of medical schemes 
in a manner that is complementary to national health policy 
• 
Investigating complaints and settling disputes in relation to the affairs of medical schemes 
• 
Collecting, disseminating information, and collaborating with stakeholders about private health care 
• 
Making recommendations and advising the Minister on the criteria for the measurement of quality and outcomes of 
health services provided by medical schemes, and related matters 
 
4. PURPOSE OF THE RFP 
4.1. The purpose of this Request for Proposal (RFP) is to invite qualified bidders to submit proposals to supply, install, and configure 
firewall appliances to replace the current 5600 firewall appliance and provide licenses of the proposed firewall for a period of three 
(3) years.

SECTION 2: TERMS OF REFERENCE/ SCOPE OF WORK  
 
1. OBJECTIVE 
1.1. The objective of this bid is to appoint a reputable bidder to submit a proposal for the supply, installation, and configuration of 
firewall appliances to replace the current Checkpoint 5600. 
 
2. SCOPE OF WORK  
2.1. Council for Medical Scheme hereby requests reputable and experienced bidders to submit proposals to supply, install, and 
configure a firewall solution to replace the current Checkpoint 5600 firewall appliance and provide licenses of the proposed firewall 
for a period of three (3) years.  
 
a) Supply, install and configure all hardware and software required. 
b) Provide hardware and software support and maintenance 365x24x7x4hrs to resolve, on-site and telephonic support, which 
includes swapping out the equipment. 
c) Provide all the licenses required immediately after installation, including licenses for 200 Virtual Private Network (VPN) for 
users. 
d) Design and configure the firewall devices according to best practices. 
e) The bidder will be required to provide any agreements they have with the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM); 
f) 
Provide installation costs along with a lead time for the completion of this project. 
g) The proposals must include any consumables that might be required to implement the proposed solutions conclusively and 
successfully. 
h) The solution must be able to integrate with the current security management solution. 
i) 
The bidder should submit a detailed project plan, including the configuration plan document and sign-off of the installation 
based on the technical specification. 
j) 
Implementation of the DMZ (Demilitarized zone) 
k) Handover after deployment with documentation of installation and configuration. 
l) 
Availability, security and operations must be across all the technology layers and OEM’s overall network security  
m) Provide certified engineers and a project manager in the appropriate technology to assist with the project implementation. 
n) Bidder should ensure that all the current policies and configurations are migrated to the new firewall solution.

ransomware attacks
