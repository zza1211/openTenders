the South African Revenue Service (SARS) as VAT vendors.  
34.2  The award of contract would be (for non-VAT vendors who included VAT in their 
prices) conditional pending the successful bidder submitting proof of registration 
as VAT vendor with SARS within 21 days of award.  
34.3  In all instances where the bidder has excluded VAT from the prices quoted, if 
the bidder is successful, the letter of appointment will clearly state that the price 
at which the contract is awarded is exclusive of VAT and that VAT will not be 
added on at any stage after the contract has been signed.

obtained. 
1.11 "Dumping" occurs when a private enterprise abroad market its goods on own initiative in the 
RSA at lower prices than that of the country of origin and which have the potential to harm the local 
industries in the RSA. 
1.12” Force majeure” means an event beyond the control of the supplier and not involving the 
supplier’s fault or negligence and not foreseeable. Such events may include, but is not restricted to, 
acts of the purchaser in its sovereign capacity, wars or revolutions, fires, floods, epidemics, quarantine 
restrictions and freight embargoes.

process or the execution of a contract to the detriment of any bidder, and includes collusive practice 
among bidders (prior to or after bid submission) designed to establish bid prices at artificial non-
competitive levels and to deprive the bidder of the benefits of free and open competition.

FOR THE APPOINTMENT OF A SERVICE PROVIDER FOR SUPPLY AND DELIVERY OF  
LAYER 3 NETWORK SWITCHES WITH POWER OVER ETHERNET (PoE +) ON AN AS AND 
WHEN REQUIRED BASIS FOR PERIOD OF THREE (3) YEARS. 
 
1. PURPOSE  
 
Mogale City Local Municipality invites reputable service providers to supply and delivery of layer 3 network 
switches with power over ethernet (PoE +) on an as and when required basis for period of three (3) years. 
 
 
2. BACKGROUND  
 
The Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Division has identified a critical need to upgrade and 
maintain the current network infrastructure.  
The required Layer 3 switches aim to deliver highly available, secure, and converged network services 
suitable for both enterprise and service provider networks. These switches should be designed to meet the 
growing demands for gigabit scalability, high availability, rich services, and multilayer switching in various 
network topologies, including backbone, distribution, wiring closet, and data centre environments. 
Exceptional gigabit scalability to support increasing network demands and High-performance packet-
forwarding capabilities to ensure efficient data transmission. Redundant components to minimize downtime 
and ensure continuous operation and Advanced failover mechanisms to maintain service continuity during 
hardware or software failures. 
 
Due to diverse network requirements, the ability to handle increasing network traffic without compromising 
performance and flexibility to expand network capacity is needed.  
 
Advanced network management features to optimize performance and security support for converged 
services, including voice, video, and data, in order to enhance communication capabilities.  
Investing in high-availability secure converged network switches is crucial for modern enterprise, these 
switches should provide the necessary scalability, performance, and rich services to support current and 
future network demands.  
Mogale City Local Municipality is currently running on Cisco Switches as a standard. 
3. SCOPE OF WORK 
 
The scope of work will include the supply and deliver of Layer 3 Managed Switch with full PoE+ and SFPs 
and Stacking Cable for Mogale City Local Municipality on an as  and when required basis  for a period of 
three (3) years. 
 
4. PROJECT SPECIFICATIONS 
 
The Full POE+ Layer 3 Switch is a dependable switch with advanced networking capabilities. With 48x 1GB 
PoE+ RJ45 ports, it provides high-speed connectivity to multiple devices while also delivering power. This 
switch also has four additional 1GB SFP ports, which allow for flexible network expansion via fibre optic 
cables. Its impressive PoE budget of up to 740W (or even more with an additional PSU) ensures that your 
powered devices get enough power without the need for additional adapters or outlets. With a switching 
capacity of 104 Gbps and a forwarding rate of up to 154.76 Mpps, this product allows for rapid data transfer 
and smooth network performance. 
Whether setting up a small office or managing larger deployments, the Switch effectively meets a wide range 
of networking needs. Key Features of Switch with 28/48 Port Full POE+ Layer 3 Switch.

[Page 42]
42
 
Full PoE+ on 24/48 Gigabit Ethernet Ports: The switch provides power directly to IP phones, wireless access
