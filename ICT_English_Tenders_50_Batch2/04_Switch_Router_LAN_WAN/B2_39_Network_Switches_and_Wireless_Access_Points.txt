CONTEXT OF THIS PROCUREMENT NEED 
 
iThemba LABS seeks to appoint a reputable service provider to supply network switches and wireless access 
points including five (5) years software maintenance support. Our existing network infrastructure is Cisco-based, 
so any proposed solution must demonstrate seamless integration through a reference document. The goal is 
to replace our aging Cisco Catalyst 3750 switches and our aging Cisco Wireless Infrastructure, which have 
reached end-of-life and pose a potential failure risk. Furthermore, the current network infrastructure does not 
meet the lab’s future needs. We require newer, faster technology that will integrate smoothly with our existing 
Cisco network infrastructure. 
 
 
 
INTERDEPENDENCIES TO OTHER SYSTEMS 
 
1. IThemba LABS has 2 x Cisco Catalyst 9500 switches connected as a Virtual Switching System (VSS) for 
redundancy. New network switches to connect to Cisco Catalyst 9500 switches core and service various 
access locations, attaching directly with fibre optic multimode short-range cables and 10G SFP. 
2. Campus switches consists of Cisco Catalyst 3650’s and 3750’s. New switches to replace these aged 
devices that are currently on the access layer. 
3. Firewall is a Fortinet Fortigate 1500D. Our Cisco Catayst 9500 switches are the core of the network and 
connect directly to the Fortigate. 
4. Cisco Wireless Infrastructure. We have a Cisco Wireless LAN Controller connected to the core and Cisco 
wireless access points connecting to various access switches.
