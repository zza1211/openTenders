Request for Proposal (RFP) for Network Access Control (NAC) System 
 
 
1. Introduction 
 
The Woodland Hills School District is seeking proposals from qualified vendors to provide a 
Network Access Control (NAC) system. The proposed solution should deliver enterprise-grade 
network security and access management capabilities similar to ExtremeControl from Extreme 
Networks. The NAC system must be fully compatible with Extreme switches, ExtremeCloud IQ, 
and Aruba Access Points. 
 
2. Background 
 
We are a K-12 school district looking to enhance our cybersecurity posture by leveraging federal 
funds through the USAC Cybersecurity Pilot Program. As part of this initiative, we aim to 
establish a multi-year agreement with a qualified vendor to implement a Network Access Control 
(NAC) system that strengthens our existing security framework. Our current infrastructure 
includes Extreme switches, ExtremeCloud IQ, Aruba Access Points, firewalls, Endpoint 
Detection and Response (EDR) solutions, and various network security tools. Our goal is to 
improve network visibility, enforce access policies, and enhance overall security compliance in 
alignment with federal and state cybersecurity mandates. We seek a vendor that can provide 
seamless integration with our existing infrastructure while delivering proactive security 
measures, role-based access control, and comprehensive threat response to safeguard 
students, staff, and district assets. 
 
3. Scope of Services 
 
The selected NAC solution should provide the following key functionalities: 
 
●​ Device Authentication & Access Control: Implement robust authentication 
mechanisms (802.1X, MAC authentication, and captive web portal-based authentication) 
for all wired and wireless devices. 
●​ Role-Based Policy Enforcement: Enable granular access control policies based on 
user roles, device types, application, and security posture. 
●​ Real-Time Network Visibility: Provide a centralized dashboard for monitoring all 
connected devices and users. 
●​ Guest & BYOD Management: Offer secure onboarding for guests and BYOD users with 
self-service or IT-managed options. 
●​ Integration with Existing Infrastructure: Seamlessly integrate with Extreme switches, 
ExtremeCloud IQ, Aruba Access Points, and existing authentication services (e.g., 
Active Directory, RADIUS, LDAP). 
●​ Compliance & Reporting: Generate detailed security compliance reports aligned with 
industry standards.

[Page 2]
●​ Scalability & High Availability: Support future growth and ensure minimal downtime 
with a resilient architecture. 
 
4. Vendor Requirements 
 
Qualified vendors must demonstrate the following: 
 
●​ Experience in deploying NAC solutions in K-12 school districts or similar environments. 
●​ Proven compatibility with Extreme Networks and Aruba wireless infrastructure. 
●​ Available 24/7 customer support and technical assistance. 
●​ References from educational institutions or similar organizations. 
●​ Vendors must be familiar with the USAC E-Rate bid submission and funding processes, 
including FCC Form 470 and Form 471 filings, the Service Provider Invoice (SPI) 
method, and the Billed Entity Applicant Reimbursement (BEAR) process. Vendors 
should demonstrate experience in successfully managing E-Rate-funded projects, 
ensuring compliance with program rules, and providing necessary documentation for 
audits and funding disbursement. 
5. Proposal Submission Requirements 
Interested vendors should include the following in their proposals: 
●​ Company Profile: Overview of the company, including history, mission, and 
organizational structure. 
●​ Solution Overview: Detailed description of the proposed NAC system and its features. 
●​ Implementation Plan: Timeline, deployment methodology, and resource requirements. 
●​ Support & Maintenance: Technical support details, SLAs, and software update policies. 
●​ Experience and References: Case studies or references from previous clients, 
particularly from similar educational institutions. 
●​ Pricing Agreement: Itemized cost breakdown, including licensing, implementation, 
training, and ongoing support. Furthermore, proposals for either a one-year or a 
three-year contract term will be considered. While a three-year price guarantee is 
preferred to ensure budgetary stability, bidders may propose a one-year contract. In 
either case, bidders should provide a clear breakdown of their annual pricing. For 
proposals offering a three-year price guarantee, the total cost should be presented as 
three equal yearly installments. Payment will be made on an annual basis. 
 
6. Evaluation Criteria 
Proposals will be evaluated based on the following factors: 
●​ Alignment with Scope of Services: Ability to meet or exceed the outlined service 
requirements. 
●​ Vendor Qualifications: Depth of experience, expertise, and resources.

[Page 3]
●​ Cost of Eligible Goods and Services: Overall pricing, including competitiveness and 
alignment with the Cybersecurity Pilot Program's eligible expenses. 
●​ Followed Bid Directions: Adherence to the proposal submission requirements, 
including completeness, clarity, and responsiveness to requested details. 
●​ Customer Service: Quality of vendor support, responsiveness, and ability to provide 
timely assistance throughout the contract period.​
Prior Experience with Product: Demonstrated success in deploying and managing the 
proposed security solutions in similar environments. 
●​ Prior Experience with Vendor: References and past performance in delivering SOC 
services to organizations of comparable size and complexity. 
●​ References: Feedback from previous clients regarding performance and service quality. 
 
7. Submission Instructions 
 
All proposals must be submitted by 4/30/2025 to Greg Roth (CITO), cyberbids@whsd.us. All 
proposals should be in PDF format, cost breakdown may be in XLS, and include "NAC 
Proposal" in the subject line. 
 
For any questions regarding this RFP, please contact Greg Roth, cyberbids@whsd.us. 
 
The Woodland Hills School District reserves the right to accept or reject any or all proposals and 
to negotiate terms with the selected vendor. We look forward to your response. 
 
Late submissions will not be considered.
