STATEMENT OF COMPLIANCE TO THE TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS 
SUPPLY, DELIVERY, AND CONFIGURATION OF THE NETWORK EQUIPMENT 
Bidders must state here either “Comply” or “Not Comply” against each of the individual parameters of each Specification, stating 
the corresponding performance parameter of the equipment offered. Statements of “Comply” or “Not Comply” must be 
supported by evidence in a Bidder Bid and cross-referenced to that evidence. A statement that is not supported by evidence 
or is subsequently found to be contradicted by the evidence presented will render the Bid under evaluation liable for 
rejection.  A statement, either in the Bidder's statement of compliance or the supporting evidence, that is found to be false 
either during Bid evaluation may be regarded as fraudulent and render the Bidder or supplier liable for prosecution, subject 
to the applicable laws and issuances.

DELIVERABLES OF THE TOURISM PROMOTIONS BOARD 
PHILIPPINES 
 
The Tourism Promotions Board (TPB) shall: 
 
1. 
Assign an end-user representative to oversee the delivery, 
inspection, and acceptance of the Network Equipments. 
 
2. 
Process payment to the supplier in accordance with the contract 
terms upon acceptance of the deliverables. 
 
3. 
Coordinate with the supplier about warranty claims, defects, or 
technical issues that may occur during the warranty period. 
 
 
TERMS AND CONDITIONS 
 
1. 
The winning bidder must fully adhere to all intellectual property 
laws, rules, and regulations.

BIDDER’S ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS 
 
1. 
Must be an IT or ICT-related company that has been operating for 
at least three (3) years and must submit a company profile. 
 
2. 
Must be a Filipino-owned, operated, and legally registered 
company under Philippine laws, with a minimum of three (3) years in 
operation. 
 
3. 
Must provide authorized reseller certificate of the proposed 
product. 
 
4. 
Must provide services through a qualified engineer or installer 
certified for the specific product. The bidder shall submit the relevant 
certification of the installer or engineer. 
 
 
CONTRACT DURATION 
 
Must be delivered within sixty (60) calendar days upon receipt of Notice 
to Proceed (NTP). 
 
 
APPROVED BUDGET FOR THE CONTRACT 
 
The Approved Budget for the Contract (ABC) is SIX HUNDRED EIGTHY 
THOUSAND PESOS ONLY (PHP 680,000.00), inclusive of all applicable 
fees and taxes, chargeable against the 2026 Approved Corporate 
Operating Budget. 
 
 
TERMS OF PAYMENT 
 
Payment shall be made through a send-bill arrangement with the Tourism 
Promotions Board (TPB) Philippines within thirty (30) days of submitting 
the Statement of Account (SOA) and supporting documents, in 
accordance with existing accounting and auditing rules and regulations. 
 
The winning bidder is encouraged to keep an account with the Land Bank 
of the Philippines (LBP) to make payments via bank deposit. If the winning 
bidder does not have an LBP account, any related bank charges will be 
the responsibility of the winning bidder.
