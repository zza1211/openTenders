            BACKGROUND TO SAEON

The South African Environmental Observation Network (SAEON) is a long-term environmental research facility
of the National Research Foundation, established in 2002 to deliver coordinated, nationwide ecological
monitoring in the wake of the 2002 World Summit on Sustainable Development. Core-funded by the Department
of Science and Innovation, SAEON comprises seven geographically dispersed research nodes and a national
office that together span South Africa’s major terrestrial, coastal and marine biomes. Through these nodes it
maintains sustained, question-driven observation systems—ranging from savanna flux towers to offshore
oceanographic moorings—designed to generate multi-decadal datasets on environmental change. SAEON’s
work is organised around three complementary pillars: environmental observation, open data management, and
education/outreach, ensuring that the knowledge  it produces informs both scientific discovery and evidence-
based policy.


   THE NEED FOR THIS PROCUREMENT WITHIN SAEON

SAEON’s  current FortiGate  firewalls  will reach End-of-Life and End-of-Support  in 2026, exposing the
organisation to escalating cyber-security and compliance risks if they are not replaced. To maintain a secure,
resilient perimeter and align with industry best practice, SAEON must refresh these appliances through a trade-
up programme to next-generation firewalls. The refresh will also incorporate a central management platform to
enable unified policy administration and reporting across all sites, and advanced AI-driven analytics to accelerate
log correlation, threat detection, and incident response. Procuring these integrated capabilities now will ensure
business continuity, reduce operational overheads, and safeguard SAEON’s scientific data assets

   CONTRACT PART A – REQUIREMENTS


             CONTRACT OBJECTIVE
 The primary objective of this contract is to engage a qualified supplier for the comprehensive supply,
 configuration, delivery, and commissioning of a modern, enterprise-grade network security solution. This
  initiative will be executed through a trade-in or equivalent technology refresh program to replace all of
 SAEON's existing perimeter firewalls before their scheduled end-of-support date in mid-2026. This timely
 replacement  is  critical  to ensuring  operational  continuity,  mitigating  security  risks associated  with
 unsupported hardware, and establishing a secure, resilient, and scalable foundation to protect SAEON's
  critical research infrastructure.

 The new solution must include an integrated central management platform that provides unified security
 policy enforcement, monitoring, and reporting across all SAEON sites. A key requirement is the incorporation
  of built-in AI capabilities, including an engine for advanced configuration analysis to optimise policies and
 AI-assisted log analytics to accelerate threat detection, correlation, and response. This Log analysis AI
  functionality must be compatible  with SAEON's  current  log analysis software  (FortiAnalyzer). The
 engagement will culminate in securing a minimum five-year contract for comprehensive vendor support,
 covering all necessary software updates, feature upgrades, and ongoing technical assistance to guarantee
 the long-term security and integrity of the network.

        COST-EFFECTIVENESS AND FLEXIBILITY

 The proposed solution must balance up-front value with long-term savings by leveraging trade-in credits,
 scalable licensing, and unified management to reduce capital and operational expenditure. Modular
 appliance models and subscription tiers should allow SAEON to right-size capacity per site and adapt
 security functions as threats evolve, while open integration points enable future add-ons without costly
 hardware swaps. This flexible, lifecycle-conscious approach ensures sustainable protection within budget
 constraints.

       INTERDEPENDENCIES TO OTHER SYSTEMS

 The replacement firewalls must accept direct migration of existing configurations—including security
  policies, object databases, VPN tunnels, and IPS profiles—from the retiring devices to avoid service
  disruption. They must also  retain  full log-export and  analytics  compatibility with SAEON’s current
 FortiAnalyzer environment, ensuring that historical reporting, dashboards, and compliance workflows
 remain intact. In addition, the new platform must integrate seamlessly with FortiEMS for centralised endpoint
 management and with deployed FortiClient agents to preserve posture checks, ZTNA enforcement, and
 automated response playbooks. Successful delivery therefore hinges on proven interoperability across
 these systems and on tooling that simplifies configuration transfer while maintaining continuity of all security
 and monitoring functions.

             SERVICES SPECIFICATION

 The appointed supplier shall deliver and commission next-generation firewall appliances, each protected by
 a  five-year advanced  hardware-replacement  warranty and a  five-year  all-inclusive  security-fabric
 subscription (intrusion prevention, web filtering, application control, DNS security, SD-WAN optimisation and
  threat-intelligence updates). A central management platform licensed for ten devices must be supplied for
  five years, including AI for configuration management, alongside a two-year subscription to an AI-driven log-
 analytics module that ingests firewall telemetry and produces actionable threat insights. Vendor-approved
 migration tooling will be used to transfer all existing firewall configurations—policies, objects, VPNs, routing,
 authentication, etc.—with pre-migration audits and post-migration validation. The scope includes hardware
  logistics, on-site delivery, rack-and-stack, software installation, cut-over planning, configuration migration,
 acceptance  testing, and handover documentation,  with 24/7 vendor-backed  technical support and
 firmware/patch management for the duration of each licence and warranty term.
