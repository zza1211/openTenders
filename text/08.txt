1.1  Introduction

The purpose of the RFQ is to invite Suppliers (hereinafter referred to as “bidders”) to submit quotes for the
Request for the procurement of Defence Information System Network (DISN) Routers with maintenance and
support of 60 months for the Department of Defence (DOD).

1.2 BACKGROUND

The Department of Defence is in a process of refreshing the network equipment and they requested SITA to
assist in procuring the Routers.

2.  Scope of Bid

2.1  Scope of Work

(a) Procurement of Routers: 1 x Core Router, 7 Distributed Routers, and 69 Access Layer 3 Routers.
(b) Maintenance and support of routers for a period of 60 months (5 years).

2.2  Delivery address

DOD Archives, Centurion.
