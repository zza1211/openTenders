SPECIFICATIONS/SCOPE OF WORK


1.1 INTENT
Howard Community College ("College") is requesting proposals from qualified Tellabs-certified
or Tellabs-experienced integrators to provide all necessary labor, project management,
configuration, integration, testing, documentation, and migration services required to replace the
College's existing Motorola Passive Optical Network (PON) equipment with the Tellabs
equipment identified in the College-provided Bill of Materials (BOM), including IEEE 802.1X
network access control on applicable interfaces.
1.2 BACKGROUND AND EXISTING ENVIRONMENT
The College's current environment consists of a Motorola PON network supporting campus
voice, data, and multicast services. This project aims to modernize the campus PON environment
by replacing active electronics in campus offices and telecommunications spaces while
maintaining and reusing the existing outside plant infrastructure wherever feasible.
The College-provided BOM is intended to replace active Motorola equipment located in offices,
classrooms in the Hickory Ridge Building, Kathleen Hetherington Hall, and other interior
facilities. Existing outside plant fiber infrastructure and associated pathways are expected to
remain in service and be reused wherever feasible.
The College intends to implement a redundant Tellabs Optical Line Terminal (OLT) architecture.
To support OLT redundancy and improved resiliency, the College will replace existing 1x32
optical splitters with 2x32 optical splitters. Splitter replacement shall support the redundant OLT
design while maintaining service continuity during migration. Existing OLT locations will
remain unchanged, and the Tellabs OLTs shall be installed in the same telecommunications
spaces currently housing the Motorola OLT equipment.
The College intends to preserve existing network services, VLAN assignments, connectivity
requirements, and operational capabilities during migration while minimizing disruption to
instructional, administrative, and student-facing services. The replacement Tellabs environment
shall support IEEE 802.1X network access control in accordance with college requirements.

1.7 SERVICE AVAILABILITY, OUTAGE, AND RECOVERY REQUIREMENTS
The Contractor shall design and execute all work to minimize impact on instructional,
administrative, public safety, and student-facing services.
Service Availability
a. No unscheduled outages shall be permitted.
b. All outages shall be coordinated with the College.
c. Maintenance windows shall be approved by the College prior to implementation.
d. The Contractor shall provide a detailed cutover plan no less than ten (10) business days prior
to each migration activity.
e. The Contractor shall identify expected service impacts for each migration event.
f. The Contractor shall provide onsite technical resources throughout all approved outage
windows.
Defined Outage
For purposes of this project, an outage shall be defined as any condition that results in:
a. Loss of voice services.
b. Loss of data connectivity.
c. Loss of multicast services.
d. Loss of 802.1X authentication services.
e. Loss of Cisco ISE integration.
f. Loss of Microsoft Intune compliance validation.
g. Degradation of service that prevents normal operations.
h. Failure of redundant OLT functionality.
The College reserves the right to determine whether service degradation constitutes an outage.
