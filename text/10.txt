1.   SCOPE

1.1    This contract makes provision for the PROVISION OF INTERNET CONNECTIVITY, SITE-TO-SITE
CONNECTION, EMAIL HOSTING AND SPAM FILTERING SERVICE FOR A PERIOD OF 5 YEARS


2.    SPECIFICATIONS

      Technical Specification

       3.1 Internet Connectivity – Main Building (Potchefstroom)

             Bandwidth: Minimum 300 Mbps symmetric (upload/download)

             Connection Type: Dedicated fiber-optic link or equivalent uncontended service

              Service Quality: Unshaped, unthrottled, and uncontented (1:1 ratio)

                Static Public IP Addresses: Minimum of five (5)

             Router and Firewall: Managed, secure devices must be provided and configured

          SLA Uptime Guarantee: ≥ 95% Yearly availability

              Latency: ≤ 10 milliseconds Internet exchange points

            Redundancy: failover link recommended
              Support: 24/7 fault reporting and remote support

        3.2 Site-to-Site Connectivity

                 3.2.1  Total Sites: 30 sites (including Ventersdorp and Potchefstroom main building
                    )(GPS locations to all sites are Below)
                 3.2.2  Bandwidth: Minimum 50 Mbps symmetric connectivity from Potchefstroom to
                     Venterdorp.  (surrounding  sites  in  Ventersdorp   will  connect  through
                     Ventersdorp’s connection to Potchefstroom). Potchefstroom Sites to have 30
                 mbps 1:3 contention ratio

                 3.2.3  Connectivity Type: EOIP Tunnel, or IPsec VPN

                 3.2.4  Security: End-to-end AES-256 encryption or equivalent

                 3.2.5  Latency Between Sites: ≤ 30 ms

                 3.2.6  Scalability: Must allow for addition/removal of sites and bandwidth changes

                 3.2.7  Quality of Service (QoS): Voice, video, and critical traffic must be prioritized

                 3.2.9  UPS: Each site to have a UPS capable of at least 30 minutes runtime for link devices

                 3.2.10 Monitoring: Centralized dashboard for IT administrators with real-time link visibility

                 3.2.11 All equipment will be on a rental basis
