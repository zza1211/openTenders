          parties in a horizontal relationship and if it involves collusive bidding (or bid rigging).² Collusive

         bidding is a pe se prohibition meaning that it cannot be justified under any grounds.

SECTION 7: SCOPE AND SPECIFICATION OF REQUIRED SUPPLY


7.1 Background

The eThekwini Municipality’s enterprise network infrastructure is expanding rapidly, driven by the increasing

adoption  of bandwidth  intensive  applications such as voice and voice  services. The Networks and

Telecommunications Department  is responsible  for supporting the enterprise network which currently

comprises of over 25 departments across more than 500 sites. To maintain consistent service delivery and

effectively respond to both planned initiatives and unforeseen needs, it is essential that the department has

the capability to procure network equipment as and when required.


7.2 Current Technical Environment

The current enterprise network comprises of over 2500 network devices. The Core Layer utilizes Cisco carrier-

grade equipment, including the Cisco ASR907 and ASR920, which support the MPLS backbone. The

Distribution Layer of the MPLS network is built on Cisco ME3400/3800 and Huawei S5720/S5732 switches.

The Campus Distribution Layer comprises Huawei S6730 switches, while the Access Layer includes Huawei

S5720/S5732 switches. Wireless  connectivity  is managed through Cisco 9800 and Huawei AC6508

controllers. The Data Centre network is currently supported by Cisco Nexus 7706 and Huawei CE6881H

switches.
