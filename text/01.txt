                              SCOPE OF WORK

     I.   The scope includes supply, installation, configuration, integration, testing and commissioning
        of two carrier-grade edge routers, two Layer-3 gateway switches, ten 25 Gbps firewall optical
       transceiver modules and all licenses, optics, adapters, cables, rack-mounting accessories and
       other components required for a complete working solution.
   II.   The proposed solution shall support two 100 Gbps NKN/ISP hand-offs, providing 200 Gbps
       aggregate nominal external bandwidth, and shall be designed for high availability without a
        single point of failure in the newly procured edge routing and gateway switching layer.
  III.   The two edge routers shall be integrated in the approved resilient dual-router design. The
        inter-router connectivity shall be configured as a 2 x 100 Gbps LAG using LACP or the
      approved equivalent mechanism.
  IV.   Each edge router shall be connected to the Layer-3 gateway switch pair through 2 x 100 Gbps
      LAG connectivity as per the approved port and optics matrix.
  V.   The Layer-3 gateway switch pair shall be configured in a resilient virtual-stack / multi-
        chassis architecture or equivalent so that downstream and upstream devices can form
        resilient LAGs across both physical switches.
  VI.    Connectivity between the Layer-3 gateway switches and the existing first firewall cluster, and
      between that firewall cluster and the campus core, shall be configured using 2 x 25 Gbps LAGs.
      The bidder shall supply/install the required 25 Gbps firewall transceiver modules and
       associated patching components.
 VII.    Connectivity between the Layer-3 gateway switches and the existing second firewall cluster,
      and between that firewall cluster and the campus core, shall be configured using 5 x 10 Gbps
      LAGs using the existing/required supported interfaces as finalized during site survey.
VIII.   The successful bidder shall carry out a detailed site survey and submit a pre-installation
      document containing the final physical/logical topology, rack and power plan, interface
       numbering, IP/routing plan, LAG mapping, VLAN/VRF mapping, optics/fiber mapping,
       configuration templates, migration sequence, rollback plan and acceptance-test plan for
       approval before implementation.
  IX.   The edge routers will be Institute-procured assets; however, operational/configuration
       control of the edge routers will remain with NIC/NKN as per the approved operational
       arrangement. The bidder shall coordinate configuration and commissioning of the routers
       with NIC/NKN and  CIC,  IIT  Kharagpur and  shall  provide  complete  configuration
       documentation and knowledge transfer.
   X.    Administrative and operational control of the Layer-3 gateway switches will remain with CIC,
        IIT Kharagpur. The bidder shall configure and integrate the switches under the direction of
      NIC/NKN and CIC engineers and shall provide complete configuration documentation and
      knowledge transfer.
  XI.    Migration from the existing last-mile edge router and gateway-switch connectivity to the new
        solution shall be planned and executed with minimum service interruption. Any outage
      window shall be taken only with prior approval of CIC and, wherever required, NIC/NKN.
 XII.   The bidder shall perform end-to-end testing after installation, including physical-interface
        status, LACP/LAG operation, IPv4/IPv6 routing, BGP/OSPF or other configured routing
        protocols,  route  convergence,  redundancy,  failover,  throughput/interface  utilization,
      management access, logging, telemetry and NMS integration as applicable.
 XIII.    High-availability and failover tests shall include failure/recovery of individual links, one
