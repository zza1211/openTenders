Instructions to Vendors



Kansas City International Academy (KCIA is looking for a company to setup and configure an
entire network. In addition to the equipment, the company should provide other technology
services for 600+ students along with 120+ staff.

We will be applying for federal ERATE funds to offset the costs of purchasing and owning a
firewall, network switches and access points. We will also be seeking reimbursement for the
“Managed Services” costs of this project, covering the portion of the service that manages the
firewall, network switches and access points. KCIA is currently at an 85% discount level for
Category II funding.  The proposal should itemize out the yearly “Managed Services” expected
cost to KCIA, which may be embedded into the total cost of the whole proposal.

Any Vendor responding to the RFP, should list multiyear options of support with the contract
proposal. KCIA also would like voluntary extensions offered in the contract proposal.

The due date for all RFP proposals will be Friday, February 13th. 2026, at 1 pm (central Time),
and will then all be opened and analyzed starting at 1:15 pm.

The proposal will go to the school board for approval on Tuesday, February 24th, 2026.

Items Requested:

A. Core Components with Managed services and monitoring using Cisco or like products:
    1.  Firewall – One on premise device, supporting a 1 gigabit internet connection. External
       monitoring service should be included to inform of us any attacks, intrusions. or other
        threats. (Currently a Cisco 2900 series device is being used).

    2. POE Switches – Nine - 48 port POE switches located in 3 closets (3 in Main closet, 3 in
       2nd floor and 2 in 3rd floor closet – currently using Meraki). For the purpose of this proposal
      assume all switch ports are fully populated.

    3.  Access Points – 51 POE access points (currently using Meraki)
            a. Main Wing (East)
                                i. Lower Level - 5
                                ii.  1st floor - 7
                               iii.  2nd floor - 12
                        iv.  3rd floor - 9
            b.  K-4 (West Wing)
                                i. 18


B. The following are additional components and services needed that would not be covered
   by the ERATE program:
        1. Hardware
            a.  Virtual Server with backup solution to handle: Printing services; Microsoft KMS
                licenses, Imaging services; DRC mandated State Testing software; Papercut type
              of Copier Software; Active Directory Services; file storage, and other server
             software components as needed.  Storage for 15 administrators on the server
                 will be needed.
            b. Phone (VOIP) system:
                                i. 90 total handsets to replace leased Cisco phones. 10 of these handsets
                    are administrative handsets.
                                ii.  Each handset should have a corresponding Direct Inward Dialing number
                     to an external number
                               iii.  All handsets should have a speaker phone with an ethernet pass thru
                       port.
                        iv. Phone system tied to 28 Wahsega Hall Speakers for bells and
                  announcements (currently InformaCast is used to do this).
