         Section I. Instructions to Bidders (ITB)


This Section specifies the procedures to be followed by Bidders in the preparation and submission of
their Bids. Information is also provided on the submission, opening, and evaluation of bids and on the
award of contract. [This Section shall not be a part of the Contract.]

                                   A. General

 1.Scope of Bid         1.1 In connection with the Request for Bids (RFB), specified in the Bid
                    Data Sheet (BDS), the Purchaser, as specified in the BDS, issues this
                       bidding document for the supply of Goods and, if applicable, any Related
                        Services  incidental  thereto,  as  specified  in  Section V,  Schedule  of
                       Requirements. The name, identification and number of lots (contracts) of
                            this RFB are specified in the BDS.

                           (a)    the term “in writing” means communicated in written form (e.g. by
                               mail, e-mail, fax, including if specified in the BDS, distributed or
                              received  through  electronic-procurement  system  used  by  the
                              Purchaser) with proof of receipt;
                          (b)     if the context so requires, “singular” means “plural” and vice versa;
                           (c)  “Day” means calendar day, unless otherwise specified as a “Business
                          Day”. A Business Day is any day that is a working day of the
                            Borrower. It excludes the Borrower’s official public holidays.

                       the BDS has applied for or received financing (hereinafter called “funds”)

                     Borrower intends to apply a portion of the funds to eligible payments under
                         the contract for which this bidding document is issued.

                        2.2 Payment by the Bank will be made only at the request of the Borrower
                     and upon approval by the Bank in accordance with the terms and conditions
                        of the Loan (or other financing) Agreement. The Loan (or other financing)
                     Agreement prohibits a withdrawal from the Loan account for the purpose of

                      Council taken under Chapter VII of the Charter of the United Nations. No
                         party other than the Borrower shall derive any rights from the Loan (or other
                         financing) Agreement or have any claim to the proceeds of the Loan (or
                         other financing).

                        3. Technical Specifications

               Data Center Network Equipment

The overall design of the data center network is built to deliver a highly available, and scalable infrastructure
that shall meet the organization's growing demands across multiple sites. The network architecture will
consist of three key locations: a primary site, a disaster recovery (DR) site, and a bunker site, each playing
a critical role in ensuring operational continuity and business resilience. The primary site will host the core
network services and applications, while the DR site will serve as a backup to ensure seamless recovery in
the event of any disruptions. The bunker site, located within the same city as the primary site, will provide
an additional layer of data redundancy, ensuring critical services remain operational even under adverse
conditions.

The network will be designed with a robust, multi-layered approach to network security, including
advanced firewalls, secure access solutions, and proactive threat prevention mechanisms to safeguard
critical data and services across all sites. The network architecture will follow a leaf-spine topology,
ensuring efficient communication across the data centers, reducing bottlenecks, and providing low-latency,
high-performance connectivity between servers, applications, and users.

The design will also leverage software-defined networking (SDN) principles, allowing for centralized
management, automation, and dynamic policy enforcement. This will enable the network to adapt rapidly
to changing  needs, optimize  traffic  flows, and ensure  flexible  resource  allocation.  Additionally,
comprehensive monitoring and reporting systems will be integrated to provide enhanced visibility into
network performance, ensuring the ability to proactively detect and address potential issues before they
impact the network’s operations.
