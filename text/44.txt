1.0 Introduction

   This Standard Bidding Document (SBD) has been prepared with a view of providing
   Consignment Agreements for the procurement of GPON FIBERHOME (ONU) AND
  HUAWEI (ONT) MODEMS.

1. The Consignment Stock Agreement shall be concluded for a period of one (1) year from
    the commencement date stated in the agreement.

2.  TelOne on a non-exclusive basis will provide facilities for product distribution and selling
    of the Products in Zimbabwe Through TelOne distribution outlets and /or agents for the
    duration of the contract.

3. The Consignor shall supply the consignment stock to TelOne at agreed quantities of each
    product, based on the orders generated for each product. To this end,

4. The Consignor shall be responsible for obtaining all licences, permits and approvals
   which are necessary or advisable for the distribution of the products in Zimbabwe and for
    the performance of TelOne.

5. The Consignor shall be responsible for obtaining all the intellectual property rights, which
    are necessary or advisable for the distribution of the Products in Zimbabwe and for the
   performance of TelOne’s duties hereunder.

6.  All sales shall be at prices and terms agreed upon and recommended by the supplier and
   TelOne.

7.  TelOne shall be entitled to a commission for the units sold and shall be negotiated at
    contract stage.

8.  Bidding  will be conducted through competitive bidding  as  specified  in  the Public
   Procurement and Disposal of Public Assets Act and Regulations and is open to all eligible
   Bidders as defined in the Regulations.

References:

References to the Act are to the Public Procurement and Disposal of Public Assets Act
[Chapter22:23] and references to the Regulations are to the Public Procurement and Disposal
of Public Assets (General) Regulations (Statutory Instrument No. 5 of 2018). The terms and
requirements in the Act and Regulations govern the submission of Bids and should be read by
all Bidders.
