1.  Introduction and background

1.1  Purpose

The purpose of this Request For Bid (RFB) is to invite Suppliers (hereinafter referred to as “bidders”) to submit
bids to appoint a service provider for the supply, installation and configuration of various LAN and WIFI
equipment for the KZN Department of Education for new sites as well as replacement of obsolete
infrastructure to be deployed across its offices in KZN as well as at schools and other government sites
identified by the Department including three-year maintenance and support for equipment installed.

1.2  Background

The KZN DOE has an outdated network and requires a phased approach to which this network will be
upgraded. The network will extend from departmental sites to schools and other education sites of relevance.
The department has set aside budget to oversee the replacement of obsolete LAN equipment and expand the
existing LAN to accommodate WIFI deployment across identified education sites.



The KZN DoE requires various LAN and WIFI equipment to be deployed across its offices in KZN as well as at
schools also in other government sites identified by the department. This deployment will take place as and
when budget becomes available. Due to the sensitive financial situation across government as well as the
tendency for the government to reprioritize its budget, the KZN Department of Education seeks to appoint a
service provider for the supply and installation of LAN and Wi-Fi equipment over a period of three years.

Having such a contract available will enable the Department to quickly procure the required equipment when
the budget is made available.



The Department envisages that once the Service Provider is appointed, the Department will then task SITA
with quantities of the required equipment at various times over a three-year period and that SITA will then
procure these devices from the successful bidder within an expeditious timeframe. In addition, SITA is required
to project manage the installation of this equipment and ensure the implementation is executed in line with
industry standards.

The Department seeks to provide a high quality of IT services by ensuring its customers have uninterrupted
access to IT systems and infrastructure. The objective is to ensure minimum downtime and be able to address
IT related faults within acceptable timeframes.
