BACKGROUND

BEC is a semi-autonomous body created by the Botswana Examinations Council Act No. 11
of 2002. The Council is mandated to apply the provisions of the Act in a manner which
promotes efficient provision of examinations services throughout the country. The Act is
available from the Government Printer in Gaborone.

BEC is committed to the use of technology to achieve its objectives, and this has resulted in
an increased reliance on Information and Communication Technology. This means  it is
continuously exposed to potential susceptibility to information security  risks. Thus, the
procurement and implementation of a Next Generation Firewall will assist BEC to reduce the
risk of exposure to such information security risks.


SCOPE

The purpose of this Request for Quotation (RFQ) is to solicit suitable companies that are
recognized partners of firewall vendors to supply, install and configure two (2) Next Generation
Firewall (NGFW) with 3-year vendor support services for firmware /software updates, technical
support and equipment warranty for each firewall to Botswana Examination Council (BEC).
